Solving Algebraic Word Problems (3-Step Method)

💡 Main Concept (Konsep Utama): Algebra helps us model real-life stories that have an unknown quantity (variable), simplify the expression, and calculate the final value once the number is revealed!

1

Step 1 — Identify the Unknown (Identifikasi Variabel)

Represent unknown quantities with a lowercase variable letter such as x, y, p, d, m.
Contoh: Misalkan x = jumlah spidol dalam 1 kotak.

2

Step 2 — Model & Simplify (Susun & Sederhanakan Bentuk Aljabar)

Convert story keywords into math operations (+, −, multiplication), and simplify using Like Terms (Suku Sejenis) or Distributive Property (Sifat Distributif) when needed.
Contoh: (3x + 5) + 2x − 2 = 5x + 3 atau 3(2y + 4) = 6y + 12.

3

Step 3 — Substitute & Calculate (Substitusi & Hitung)

Replace the variable with the given number and compute the final answer carefully.
Contoh: Jika x = 12, maka 3(12) + 4 = 36 + 4 = 40.
